# Product Scope

## Product Vision

Analiza Intelligence is a corporate Business Intelligence platform for Analiza operations in Central America. It centralizes, validates, analyzes, and visualizes operational and financial data across countries, companies, branches, managers, professionals, services, and data sources.

The platform must not depend on Power BI. It should support future companies and units without reworking the core architecture.

## Initial Business Units

- Analiza Fisioterapia
- Analiza Laboratorio
- Analiza Imagenes

## Initial Countries

- Guatemala
- Belice
- El Salvador
- Honduras
- Nicaragua
- Costa Rica
- Panama

Each country will support its own currency, time zone, date format, tax placeholders, branches, enabled companies, targets, service catalogs, and data sources.

Phase 2 creates navigation entries for all core product areas. Modules scheduled for later phases route to protected placeholders instead of public 404 pages.

## Non Goals For Early Phases

- No public self-registration.
- No production deployment without explicit authorization.
- No identifiable clinical information in executive dashboards.
- No net income calculation until complete operating expenses exist.
- No AI-dependent KPI calculation.
- No automatic KPI extraction from PDF unless an approved parser exists.
